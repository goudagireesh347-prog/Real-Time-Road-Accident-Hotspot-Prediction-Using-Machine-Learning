Technical Explanation for Project Viva / Evaluation

Why Random Forest Classifier?

Random Forest is an ensemble meta-estimator that fits an array of 120 decorrelated decision trees on diverse subsamples of the geospatial dataset. By averaging individual tree votes, it curtails overfitting, captures complex nonlinear interactions between traffic flow, weather, and road geometry, and performs robustly in the presence of noise without requiring feature scaling.

What do the Feature Importances show?

The top contributing factors identified are Traffic Volume, Hour of Day, Latitude/Longitude Coordinates, and Weather Conditions. This mirrors empirical highway safety research: congestion combined with peak rush hours and adverse monsoon traction represents the primary tipping point for vehicular collisions.
